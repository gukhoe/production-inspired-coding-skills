# Licensing boundaries

MIT licensing applies to the independently developed auditor code.
The root LICENSE lists the covered paths. The installable auditor package contains
that code and its report assets; it does not bundle the 16 original skill texts or
research archives.

Original attachments and research inputs in sources/, evidence/ and benchmarks/
retain their recorded provenance. No new permission for those materials is inferred
from the auditor's license. The 16 revised skill procedures derive from the supplied
original skills and are not automatically relicensed by this change. Comparator
material retains comparators/LICENSE. Public availability is not a substitute for
checking the applicable license or obtaining permission for a particular use.
